# Acme Support Handbook

Acme offers a 30-day refund policy on all annual plans. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time but are not refunded.

Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm UTC. Priority support customers get a response within 2 hours; standard customers within 24 hours.

To reset your password, go to Settings > Security and click "Reset password". A link is emailed to you and expires after 15 minutes.
